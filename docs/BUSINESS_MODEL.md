# BUSINESS MODEL & UNIT ECONOMICS — CONSTRUCTOPS
**Target Segment:** Small & Medium Commercial/Residential Contractors & Subcontractors  
**Geography:** Emerging Construction Markets (South Asia / MENA / Pakistan)  

---

## 1. Problem Statement
- **Material Theft & Ghost Discrepancies:** Uncontrolled material deliveries and wastage cost contractors 8%–15% of project margins.
- **Labor Ghost Billing:** Manual attendance registers and paper muster sheets cause daily wage leakage.
- **Uncontrolled Budget Drift:** Owners find out a project exceeded its budget weeks after subcontractor payments are made.
- **Fragmented WhatsApp Reporting:** Daily site photos and progress logs get buried in chat threads with zero structured data.

---

## 2. Value Proposition
- **Real-Time Financial Control:** Instant reconciliation of site expenditure against approved line-item budgets.
- **Audited Inventory Ledger:** Every bag of cement and ton of steel has a timestamped IN/OUT record tied to site supervisors.
- **Automated Workforce Muster:** Daily attendance logs with automatic daily wage liability calculation.
- **Decision-Ready Executive Dashboard:** Instant oversight across multi-site operations from a centralized web interface.

## 4. Unit Economics Assumptions (12-Month Projections)
- **Target CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Rs 25,000 (Direct B2B sales + local contractor associations).
