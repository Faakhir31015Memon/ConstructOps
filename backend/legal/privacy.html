Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data ConstructOps ("we", operated by Faakhir Memon, Pakistan) collects through its website and SaaS platform, why we collect it, how it is protected, and what rights you have. It applies to account holders ("Customers") and to the site-team individuals whose records Customers enter into the platform.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Account & company data

  • Name, email address, hashed password, role and language preference of each user.
  • Company name and workspace configuration.
  • Subscription records: plan, payment proofs, bank transaction references (e.g. TRX IDs), and the name/account number you voluntarily include in payment notes.

1.2 Operational data entered by Customers

  • Project, budget, expense, material, contract and payment records.
  • Worker and contractor records entered by Customers — names, contact numbers, CNIC where the Customer chooses to record it, attendance and wage data.
  • Site photos and daily reports uploaded by Customers.

For this category ConstructOps acts as a data processor on the Customer's behalf; the Customer is responsible for lawfully collecting and entering this data.

1.3 Technical data

  • Log data: IP address, browser/device information, request timestamps and error traces needed for security and reliability.
  • Browser storage (localStorage) for session tokens and interface language. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

3. How We Use Data

  • Providing the Service: dashboards, reports, exports, alerts and the in-app assistant.
  • Account authentication, subscription verification and fraud prevention.
  • Sending transactional messages you requested: password resets, digests (WhatsApp/SMS/email where enabled), payment verification notices.
  • Improving reliability: aggregated, non-identifying statistics and error monitoring.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not use your project data to train third-party AI models; assistant answers are computed from your own tenant-scoped workspace data.

4. Legal Basis

We process personal data on the basis of (a) performance of our contract with you (the Terms of Service), (b) your consent where consent is the appropriate basis (e.g. optional digests), and (c) legitimate interests in securing and operating the Service.

6. Data Retention

  • Account and workspace data: retained while your company is active.
  • After a Customer deletes a company workspace, related records (projects, workers, reports, photos) are cascade-deleted from the primary database; automatic backups age out and are purged within a maximum of 14 backup cycles.
  • Subscription/legal records (invoices, payment proofs) are retained as required for financial and tax compliance.
  • Server logs: up to 12 months.

7. Security

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed; sessions use short-lived tokens with revocation. All API traffic is TLS-encrypted. Tenant isolation, field whitelisting, injection sanitization, rate limiting and signed payment webhooks are enforced in the platform. No system is perfectly secure — report concerns to the contact below and we will act promptly.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable Pakistani law, you may request: access to your personal data, correction, deletion of your account, or a portable export of your workspace (available anytime via PDF/Excel export and account deletion). Workers or contractors whose data was entered by a Customer should ask that Customer to correct or remove it. To exercise rights, email us — we respond within 30 days.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at persons under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We will post updates to this page and note the effective date; material changes are also announced in the app.
