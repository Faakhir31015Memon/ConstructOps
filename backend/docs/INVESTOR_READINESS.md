# INVESTOR READINESS & DUE DILIGENCE PACKAGE — CONSTRUCTOPS
**Company:** ConstructOps (PropTech / ConTech SaaS)  
**Stage:** Pre-Seed / Seed Prototype & Traction Foundation  

---

## 1. Executive Briefing
ConstructOps empowers construction contractors to eliminate operational blindspots, prevent material theft, enforce labor attendance accountability, and protect project profit margins through a specialized, multi-tenant cloud workspace.

---

## 2. Investment Highlights
1. **Large Underserved Market:** The construction sector is one of the least digitized industries globally, relying on manual paper notebooks and unstructured WhatsApp messages.
2. **Clear SaaS Unit Economics:** High Annual Contract Value (ACV) with strong retention potential due to high switching costs once an ongoing project's financial ledger is centralized in ConstructOps.
3. **Multi-Tenant Foundation:** Production-hardened tenant data isolation, secure authentication, role-based access control, and audited transactional ledgers.
4. **Pragmatic AI Moat:** Context-aware site risk analysis designed for real construction site challenges rather than superficial AI gimmicks.
