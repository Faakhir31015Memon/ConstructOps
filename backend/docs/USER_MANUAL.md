# ConstructOps - End-User Manual

A simple guide for site owners, managers, accountants and contractors. You do not need any technical skill to use ConstructOps. Everything works in a web browser, on a phone or a computer.

## 1. What ConstructOps does

ConstructOps keeps your construction business in one place. You create a project (a site), then record its budget, expenses, material stock, workers, daily attendance, daily photo reports and contractor payments. The app checks your data and raises alerts when something looks wrong — a cost crossing the budget, a negative material balance, or a theft risk. Payroll comes straight from attendance, so there is no separate wage register and no month-end argument. Reports and invoices download as Excel or PDF. You pay once per period: a 15-day free trial, then a 1, 6 or 12 month plan.

### Roles and what each one can see

There are four roles inside your company, plus the ConstructOps admin team. Your role is decided when the account is made.

- **Owner** — the person who registers the company. Sees everything: the Company Dashboard, all projects, all money. Can create projects, approve or reject expenses, create contracts, pay contractors, assign contractors to sites, use the WhatsApp Digest, and open the Audit trail.
- **Manager** — runs the sites. Sees the Company Dashboard and all projects. Can create projects, add workers, mark attendance, file daily reports, log materials and expenses, approve or reject expenses, mark a project complete, and manage contractor assignments. Cannot pay contractors from the Contracts tab and cannot open the Audit trail.
- **Accountant** — handles the money. Sees the Projects list and each project's pages, but the app hides the "Add Worker Profile", "Upload Daily Log", "Register Material" and stock buttons for this role. Can disburse contractor payments and sees the "Billing history" under **Account & Security**. Cannot approve expenses (only Owner/Manager do that) and cannot open the Company Dashboard.
- **Contractor** — your subcontractor or worker foreman. Logs in with an account you create for them. Sees only the projects you assign ("My Assigned Construction Sites"). Can file daily reports, log materials, add workers and attendance on assigned sites, submit expenses for approval ("Submit Expense for Approval"), and view their own contract, payment history and receipt PDFs. Cannot see other projects, the dashboard, or the company's other costs.
- **ConstructOps admin** — the platform team. Verifies your subscription payments and issues receipts.

Contractor note: "Contractors" are people with a login. "Workers" (masons, helpers, carpenters) sit in the Worker Registry and do **not** have logins — you mark their attendance for them.

## 2. Getting started

### 2.1 Create your company account (the Owner does this)

1. Open the ConstructOps website. Click **Create Account** under "Don't have an account?".
2. Fill in the four fields: **Owner Name**, **Company/Builder Name** (for example "ABC Builders"), **Email Address**, and **Password**.
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. Your company starts with a **15-day free trial** automatically. You do not pay anything now, and no card details are asked for.
5. You land on the "All Construction Projects" page. It is empty — that is normal.

### 2.2 Follow the onboarding checklist

1. On the Projects page you will see a card titled **Set up ConstructOps** with a progress bar and "X of 4 steps done".
2. The four steps are: add a site, register workers, file a daily report, log an expense. Each step shows a link such as **Add your first site →**.
3. Tick the steps as you go. The card hides itself when all steps are done, or click the **X** in its corner to hide it for now.
4. The card also shows your trial days left and how much of your plan's limits you have used (sites and users).

### 2.3 Add your first project (site)

1. On the Projects page, click **New Project** (top right).
2. The "Initialize New Project" window opens. Fill in:
   - **Project Site Name** — e.g. "House No. 45 Phase 6"
   - **Location City/Address** — e.g. "DHA, Karachi"
   - **Allocated Budget (PKR)** — e.g. 5000000
   - **Start Date** and **Target End Date** (the start date cannot be after the end date)
3. Click **Create Project**.
4. Your site now shows as a card with **Budget**, **Spent**, **Remaining** (green if inside budget, red if over), **Ends** date and a **Completion Progress** bar. Click **Enter Site Dashboard** on the card to open the site's workspace.

### 2.4 Signing in later

1. Open the site and enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
2. Click **Log In**. Everyone lands on the Projects page; owners and managers can then open **Company Dashboard** from the left menu.
3. The left menu (sidebar) holds: **Company Dashboard** (owner/manager), **Account & Security**, **Projects** (contractors see **My Assigned Projects**), **Contractors** (owner/manager), and **Log Out** at the bottom. On a phone, tap the menu icon (three lines) at the top left to open it.

## 3. Day-to-day work

Open a project with **Enter Site Dashboard**. The project header always shows **Approved Spent** against budget, **Pending Due** (bills waiting for approval), **Remaining Budget** and **Completion %**. Below it are the tabs: **Overview**, **Daily Reports**, **Workers & Attendance**, **Material Stock**, **Expenses Ledger**, **Contracts & Payments**, **Payroll**, and for owners/managers **WhatsApp Digest** and **Contractor Assignments**.

### 3.1 Company Dashboard and alerts

1. Click **Company Dashboard** in the left menu (owner/manager/admin).
2. The top cards show **Active Projects**, **Labor Strength** (workers registered), **Materials Used** and **Total Outflow** (rupees spent across sites).
3. Two charts follow: **Project Completion Progress** (percent complete per site) and **Budget vs Actual Cost** (dashed blue = Planned, orange = Actual).
4. The "Detailed System Audits" panel has buttons: **Projects**, **Expenditures**, **Daily Logs**, **Labor Directory** (and **Users Management**, **Subscription Payments**, **Activation** for the ConstructOps admin). Each shows a table across all your sites.
5. Alerts appear in two places:
   - The **bell icon** at the top right of every page. A red number counts unread alerts. Click it to open "Alert Notifications", then click the **check mark** on an alert to mark it read. The list refreshes by itself every 20 seconds.
   - Inside each project's **Overview** tab, under **Active Alerts & Theft Checks** (e.g. over-budget or negative stock warnings). Click **Dismiss** to clear one.
6. To finish a project: on the project header click **Complete** (owner/manager) and confirm "mark this project as 100% completed".

### 3.2 Expenses (the money going out)

1. Open the **Expenses Ledger** tab. The tab name shows "(N pending)" while bills await approval.
2. Click **Record Expense** (contractors see **Submit Expense for Approval**).
3. In "Record Expense Item" choose the **Expense Category** (Raw Material, Labor Wages, Machinery, Transport, Subcontractor, Utilities, Govt Permits, Site Safety, or Miscellaneous), type **Amount (PKR)** and a **Bill Description** (e.g. "Purchased 10 cement bags").
4. Click **Log Bill**. Contractor and site-team entries arrive as **pending**.
5. The owner or manager reviews them in the same table: click **Approve** (green) or **Reject** (red) on the row. A rejected bill shows its "Reason:".
6. Only **approved** expenses count toward the **Approved Spent** figure on the project header and Overview. Pending bills sit under **Pending Due**.
7. Use the **Category Filter** pills (All Categories, Material, Labor, Machinery, Transport, Subcontractor, Utilities, Permits, Safety, Misc) to filter the table.

### 3.3 Materials (stock in and out)

1. Open the **Material Stock** tab.
2. First register the items you buy: click **Register Material**, type the **Material Name** (Cement, Bricks, Steel…) and choose the **Inventory Unit** — Bags (cement), Kilograms (steel/nails), Tons (aggregate) or Pieces (bricks/tiles). Click **Register**.
3. Every day, record stock movements: click **Log Stock Transaction**.
4. Choose the **Select Material**, then the **Transaction Type**: **Stock Incoming (IN)** when a delivery arrives, or **Usage on Site (OUT)** when it is consumed. Enter the **Quantity** and an optional **Auditing Notes** (supplier bill reference). Click **Log Audit Transaction**.
5. Each material card shows **Inward Total**, **Used Total** and **Remaining Balance**. If remaining goes below zero the card turns red and shows "Discrepancy warning triggered!" — an alert that material may be missing.
6. The table below, "Stock Modification Audits (Fraud Prevention)", lists every IN/OUT entry with who logged it ("Authorized By") and when. Nothing can be changed quietly.

### 3.4 Workers

1. Open the **Workers & Attendance** tab.
2. Click **Add Worker Profile**.
3. Fill in **Worker Full Name**, **Role / Trade** (Mason, Helper, Carpenter…), **Daily Wage Rate (PKR)** and **Phone Number**. Click **Save Profile**.
4. The table lists every worker with name, role, daily wage, phone and today's attendance state.

### 3.5 Attendance

1. In the **Workers & Attendance** tab, pick the day with the date box next to "Labor Force Registry".
2. Fast way at the gate: click **Mark all present** (or **All absent**). A confirmation strip appears — click **Confirm** to apply it to the whole list, or **Cancel** to back out. Then fix the few exceptions by clicking **Present** or **Absent** on each worker's row.
3. No internet? Attendance is saved on the phone and the tab shows a badge "N waiting to sync". It syncs by itself when the connection returns ("No signal — saved on this device and will sync automatically").
4. Attendance is what payroll is built from — mark it every day.

### 3.6 Daily site reports (with photos)

1. Open the **Daily Reports** tab and click **Upload Daily Log** (contractors, owners and managers can post; the button is hidden for the accountant).
2. In "Daily Work Log" choose the **Work Stage / Type**: Foundation work, Structure building, Slab casting, Finishing & painting, or Other.
3. Write the **Work Description Log** — what happened today, any issues, what was completed.
4. Under "Site Capture (Upload from Gallery)" click **Choose Photo(s)** and pick photos from your phone. Rules: each photo must be **under 5 MB**, up to **6 files are uploaded per pick**, and a report holds up to **10 photos**. You can pick again to add more. Tap the small **×** on a thumbnail to remove a photo.
5. Click **Submit Daily Log**.
6. Under each report you will see "Site Vision AI Analysis Output:" — an automatic note about progress and a delay-risk warning (the box turns red when a delay risk is found).

### 3.7 Contractors, contracts and contractor payments

Do this in order:

**Step A — create the contractor's login** (owner/manager):

1. Click **Contractors** in the left menu.
2. Click **Add Contractor**. Fill in **Full Name**, **Email Address** and a **Temporary Password** (at least 6 characters), then click **Create Account**. Share the password with the contractor securely.

**Step B — assign them to a site:**

3. On the Contractors page click **Assign to Project** (or **Assign Project** on a contractor's card).
4. Choose the **Select Contractor** and **Select Project**, then click **Grant Access**. The contractor now sees only that site under "My Assigned Construction Sites".
5. To remove access later, click the shield-off icon on the project tag in their card, or in the project's **Contractor Assignments** tab click **Revoke Access**.

**Step C — write the contract** (owner/manager, inside the project's **Contracts & Payments** tab):

6. In the "Contract Agreement" card fill **Contract Amount (PKR)** and **Terms & Conditions** (scope of work, milestones), then click **Save Contract Terms**. A contractor must be assigned to the site first.

**Step D — pay them** (owner or accountant only):

7. In the "Disburse Contractor Payment" card fill **Payment Amount (PKR)**, choose the **Payment Method** (Bank Transfer / IBFT, Cheque, Cash, Other), and enter the **Reference / Cheque Number**. Click **Record Disbursed Payment**.
8. The "Payment Disbursements History" table shows all payments with a running **Total Disbursed**. Click **PDF** on a row to download that payment's invoice/receipt.

**What the contractor sees:** the same tab titled "My Contract & Payment Statement" with cards **Contract Value**, **Total Received**, **Balance Due** and **Instalments**, plus "My Payment History" where each row has a **PDF** receipt button.

### 3.8 Payroll panel

1. Open the project's **Payroll** tab.
2. Pick the month with the month box (defaults to the current month).
3. The cards show **Workers on roster**, **Days paid** and **Gross wages**.
4. The table lists each worker: **Daily wage**, **Present** days, **Absent** days and **Amount**. Wages are calculated from attendance: days present × daily wage. There is nothing to enter by hand.
5. Click **Statement PDF** to download the monthly payroll statement.

### 3.9 WhatsApp daily digest (owner/manager)

1. Open the project's **WhatsApp Digest** tab.
2. Type the numbers under "WhatsApp numbers (one per line, +92 format)", e.g. `+92 300 1234567`.
3. Tick **Send automatically every evening for this site** to get the digest daily (the demo shows it arrives around 9 PM with budget, labour, material and progress figures).
4. Click **Save settings**.
5. Click **Preview** to see the exact message without sending it, or **Send now** to push it to the saved numbers immediately.
6. A status pill shows "WhatsApp connected" or "WhatsApp not configured". If it is not configured you still get the preview only.

## 4. Exports: reports, statements and invoices

1. **Full project report:** on any project's header, under the project name, use the "Download full project detail (Excel / PDF)" buttons: click **Excel** or **PDF**. The button shows "Preparing..." and then the downloaded file name appears next to it.
2. **Company-wide report:** on the Dashboard, use the "Full company report (all projects)" **Excel** / **PDF** buttons at the top right.
3. **Payroll statement:** Payroll tab → **Statement PDF**.
4. **Contractor payment receipt:** Contracts & Payments tab → on any payment row click **PDF** (works for both the company view and the contractor's own view).
5. **Subscription receipt:** open **Account & Security** in the left menu → "Billing history" table → click **Receipt** on any approved payment row.
6. Files download through your normal browser downloads. If a button shows "Retry", the document could not be generated — click it again or check your connection.

## 5. Subscriptions and payments

### 5.1 Your trial and your plan

1. Every new company gets a **15-day free trial**. The top bar shows a badge like **15d Trial (Xd left)**. Click the badge any time to open the plan window.
2. When 7 days or less remain, a yellow banner appears: "Subscription Alert: Plan Expiring in X Days!" — click **Renew / Extend Plan**.
3. Plans (prices shown inside the app, in PKR):
   - **1 Month Plan** — 30 days access
   - **6 Months Plan** — 180 days access (marked MOST POPULAR)
   - **12 Months Plan** — 365 days access (marked BEST VALUE; includes a site and team-member allowance shown on the card)

### 5.2 Choosing a plan

1. Click the subscription badge in the top bar, the banner button, or **Reactivate Account** if already expired.
2. The "Manage Subscription Plan" window shows the three plan cards. Click the card you want (the chosen card gets an orange border).
3. Click **Proceed to Payment**.

### 5.3 Paying by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa (manual verification)

1. On the "Subscription Payment — PKR" step choose your method: **Bank Transfer**, **EasyPaisa** or **JazzCash** (a **Debit / Credit Card** option is also listed).
2. The **How to pay** box shows the account numbers/instructions to send money to. Send the payment yourself first (from your bank app, JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet).
3. Then report it in the window:
   - **Bank Transfer:** choose **Bank (Pakistan)** from the list, fill **Account Title**, **Account Number** (or **IBAN** — one of the two is required), and the **Transaction / UTR Reference Number**.
   - **EasyPaisa / JazzCash:** fill the **Transaction ID (TID)** from your payment.
4. Click **Submit Rs ... Payment** (the button shows the plan price).
5. The window changes to "Payment Under Verification" with an hourglass. Click **Go to Login Screen**.
6. A ConstructOps admin checks your payment against the bank/wallet record and approves it. As soon as it is approved your login works again, and the login screen shows "Payment submitted successfully! You will be able to log in as soon as the admin approves your payment." until then.
7. If some gateways are switched on, a green **Pay Live via JazzCash / Pay Live via Easypaisa** button also appears; it opens the wallet's checkout page, and access unlocks automatically once that payment is captured.

### 5.4 Where to see your payment status and receipts

1. Left menu → **Account & Security** → "Billing history". Rows show **Plan**, **Amount**, **Status** (pending / approved / rejected) and a **Receipt** PDF button for approved payments.
2. If a payment is **rejected**, the admin's reason shows in the ConstructOps admin's list and your submission stays pending until you pay again correctly.

### 5.5 What happens when a plan expires

1. The day your trial or plan ends, the account is **suspended automatically**. A red banner appears: "Account Access Suspended:".
2. Inside the app you get a lock screen titled "Workspace Suspended": "Your 15-day free trial or ConstructOps subscription plan has ended." Your data is safe — nothing is deleted.
3. Click **⚡ Reactivate ConstructOps Now** (or **Reactivate Account** on the banner), choose a plan, pay and submit as in section 5.3. After admin approval everything continues exactly where you left off.
4. Team members cannot log in while the account is suspended.

## 6. The AI assistant

1. A round orange chat button floats at the bottom right of every page. Click it to open the **ConstructOps Assistant**.
2. The subtitle says it all: "Answers from your own workspace". It answers only from **your** company's data — budgets, approvals, alerts, workers and billing. It never shows another company's data.
3. On the first open it shows example question chips — click one or type your own question (for example "Which site is closest to its budget?" or "How many expenses are pending approval?"). Press **Enter** or the send arrow to ask.
4. The assistant replies with short bullet answers. It understands your interface language (see section 7).
5. Your role decides what the assistant can tell you: contractors only get answers about their assigned projects and their own submitted expenses; accountants and managers get what their normal pages show; owners see the whole company.
6. The circular arrow icon in the assistant's header clears the conversation. The last few messages are kept on your device between visits.

## 7. Language settings

1. At the top right of every page there is a language dropdown (next to your name). It offers three choices:
   - **English**
   - **Roman Urdu** (Urdu words written with English letters, e.g. "Site ka budget cross ho gaya")
   - **اردو** (Urdu script; the whole page flips to right-to-left)
2. Pick one and the interface changes immediately.
3. The choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to any other device, and it is remembered when you log in again.
4. The AI assistant also answers in the language you picked.

## 8. Troubleshooting and FAQ

**Q1. I forgot my password.**
1. On the login screen click **Forgot password?**.
2. On "Reset your password" enter your **Email Address** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open your email and click the one-time link. (If the app shows **Open the reset link**, use it.)
4. On "Choose a new password" type the new password twice (they must match) and submit. You are returned to login with "Password updated. Please sign in with your new password."

**Q2. I changed my password and now I am logged out everywhere.**
That is by design. Changing a password on **Account & Security** ends all other sessions. Log in again on each device. To do the same on purpose, use **Sign out of all devices**.

**Q3. My account says it is suspended / banned.**
- "Workspace Suspended" = your trial or plan expired. Pay a plan (section 5) and wait for admin approval.
- If your ConstructOps admin banned the user (Users Management → **Ban User**), that person cannot log in until the admin clicks **Unban User**. Contact your company owner or the ConstructOps admin.

**Q4. I submitted my payment but login is still blocked.**
The status is "Payment Under Verification". Nothing is wrong — an admin must approve the transfer. Log in once it is approved. The yellow lock screen and the login message both confirm your payment was received. If approved but still locked, sign out (**Log Out** in the menu) and sign in again.

**Q5. Photos will not upload with my daily report.**
- Each photo must be **under 5 MB**. The app says "Each photo must be under 5 MB" and stops the upload. Use your phone camera's smaller size option, or crop the photo.
- Each pick uploads up to **6 files**; a report holds up to **10 photos**. To add more than 6, press **Choose Photo(s)** again.
- If it still fails, check your internet connection and try once more.

**Q6. Attendance did not save — no signal at site.**
No problem. Tap **Present/Absent** or **Mark all present** as usual; the app marks "N waiting to sync" and stores it on the device. When internet returns it syncs automatically and shows "Attendance saved — X new, Y updated."

**Q7. The Excel/PDF export button shows "Retry" or a spinner.**
Reports are built on the server when you click. Wait a few seconds and click again. If it keeps failing, check the connection; a signed-out session redirects to the login page ("Your session ended. Please sign in again.").

**Q8. A contractor cannot see a project.**
Their account only shows sites they are assigned to. Ask the owner/manager: **Contractors** page → **Assign to Project** → select the contractor and the project → **Grant Access**.

**Q9. The worker payroll figure looks wrong.**
Payroll = days present × daily wage. Open **Workers & Attendance**, pick the missing dates and correct the **Present/Absent** marks; the **Payroll** tab updates when you reload the month.

**Q10. How do I log out safely?**
Click **Log Out** at the bottom of the left menu. On a shared computer this ends the saved session on the server, so nobody else can re-open your workspace from that browser.
